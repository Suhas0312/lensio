PD is on your prescription (sometimes written “PD 63”).

Precise PD with a card
  1. Take off your glasses. Hold any bank, ATM or ID card flat against your forehead, just above your eyebrows, long side across.
  2. Face the camera straight, about an arm's length away, eyes open, looking at the lens.
  3. Tap Take photo (3-second countdown), or use a photo from your gallery.

All bank/ID cards are exactly 85.6 mm wide (ISO standard), so the card gives a true millimetre scale. Nothing is uploaded.

Drag the two red lines exactly onto the left and right edges of the card. Check the blue dots sit in the centre of each pupil (drag to fix).

“Use this photo” measures your face from it and shows the frames in your size. Take it facing the camera, without glasses.
